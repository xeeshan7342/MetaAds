      What the doc can look like
      • Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx, every tab), CSV, text or Markdown. From Google Docs use File, Download, Microsoft Word (.docx); from Sheets use .xlsx. Or use Paste text.
      • Campaigns from a table (Campaign | Objective | Budget | Ad Sets) or headings like Campaign 1: Intro Offer. Ad sets as headings, bold lines (Ad Set 2: Lookalike 1%), table rows or plain names before their targeting.
      • Targeting the tool picks up: Location: Austin, TX + 15 mile radius, Age: 25-45, Gender: Women, Interests: yoga, pilates, Custom audience, Lookalike, Exclude, Placements: Instagram Feed and Stories, and free text such as Audience: women 30 to 55 interested in fitness.
      • Ad text as lists, numbered lines (Primary Text 2: ...), Option 1: lines, one block per ad, or an ad table (Ad | Primary Text | Headline | CTA). Primary text can run over several lines.
      • Every objective with its conversion location as Ads Manager names it: Engagement – Video views, Engagement – Messages on WhatsApp, Engagement – Facebook Page visits, Leads (Instant Form), Traffic to Instagram profile, App installs, Awareness.
      • Campaign settings: Objective, budgets (daily, monthly or lifetime, campaign or ad set level), Special ad category, Bid strategy: Cost cap $25, Conversion event, start and end dates, Page, pixel and lead form IDs, UTM parameters.
      • In a doc that also plans Google or other platforms, only the Meta sections are read. Anything the tool cannot place shows up in the import report, where you can teach it.

          Import in Ads Manager

          1. In Ads Manager, open the Import and export menu above the table and choose Import ads in bulk.
          2. Pick the .xlsx (or CSV). Ads Manager checks every row and lists anything it cannot read.
          3. Fix or skip flagged rows, then Import. Everything arrives with the status you picked above.
          4. Work through the After import list: interests, audiences, creatives and city radius.
          5. Check one ad set's locations and one ad's preview, then publish.

          First time with a new ad account? Import one campaign first to confirm the columns, or load your account's blank template above.

          Meta lets an ad account publish imported campaigns only after several weeks of running ads that follow its policies (error #3738001). On a new account, build the first campaigns by hand with the values shown here, and import later.
